2. Platform Scope & Workspace Access

RenewalTrack provides automated renewal tracking, contract lifecycle management, notification scheduling, and team collaboration tooling. Access is provisioned based on assigned organizational roles:

  • Primary Administrator: Holds administrative jurisdiction over workspace configurations, member invites/revocations, SMTP dispatch credentials, and security audit logs.
  • Employees & Viewers: Authorized to monitor, update, and manage designated renewal records, upload vendor agreements, and review upcoming renewal deadlines according to organizational permissions.

3. Automated Renewal Notifications & Schedules

RenewalTrack dispatches automated alert emails (at customizable intervals such as 90, 60, 30, 14, 7, and 1-day milestones) and in-app notifications. While RenewalTrack maintains continuous dispatch daemons, your organization retains ultimate responsibility for maintaining accurate contract renewal dates, valid recipient email addresses, and taking timely commercial renewal action.

4. Account Security & Credentials

Users must provide accurate, current, and complete registration details. You are responsible for safeguarding your login credentials and preventing unauthorized access to your workspace. Any suspected credential compromise or unauthorized access must be reported to the workspace Primary Administrator immediately.

5. Data Ownership & System Integrity

You retain full intellectual property ownership of all contract metadata, proprietary vendor records, and financial renewal figures uploaded to your workspace. You agree not to upload malicious scripts, attempt unauthorized database injection, or interfere with background dispatch workers.

6. Limitation of Liability

RenewalTrack is provided on an "as is" and "as available" basis. Under no circumstances shall RenewalTrack or its administrators be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages resulting from third-party vendor auto-renewals, SMTP delivery delays beyond our control, or external network downtime.

Privacy Guaranteed Compliant with Modern Privacy Standards

1. Information We Collect

RenewalTrack collects only information necessary to deliver contract renewal tracking and workspace collaboration:

  • Account Profile: Full name, verified business email, contact phone number, and cryptographic password hash.
  • Renewal & Vendor Data: Contract titles, vendor names, renewal dates, cost metrics, billing cycles, and uploaded agreement attachments.
  • Security Audit Logs: Timestamped action records, authenticated IP addresses, and session activities recorded in our tamper-resistant audit trail.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to provide, maintain, and protect your RenewalTrack services:

  • Delivering automated renewal reminders, digest notifications, and email alerts before deadlines.
  • Authenticating user sessions and enforcing role-based permissions (Admin, Employee, Viewer).
  • Generating workspace cost forecasts, spend analytics, and compliance audit histories.

3. Security & Cryptographic Protection

We enforce industry-standard security safeguards to protect your confidential corporate data:

  • User passwords are protected using salted scrypt key-derivation algorithms and are never stored in plaintext.
  • Authentication tokens are securely signed with HMAC-SHA256 signatures and session-bound.
  • Data access is strictly isolated by workspace, preventing unauthorized cross-tenant visibility.

4. Third-Party Sharing & Data Selling

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your corporate contract data, user contacts, or renewal records to advertisers or third-party brokers. Data is transmitted solely to operational subprocessors (such as your organization's configured SMTP mail server) necessary for system notifications.

5. Data Retention & User Rights

You and your workspace administrator have the right to inspect, export (via CSV/ICS/JSON), update, or delete your data at any time. When an account or renewal record is permanently removed by an administrator, related records and associated reminder queues are deleted from the active datastore.
